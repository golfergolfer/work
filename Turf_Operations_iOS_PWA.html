Install as iOS App on iPhone

You can save this application directly to your iPhone's home screen so it runs full-screen, without Safari browser bars, completely offline on the golf course.

  1. 1 Tap the Safari Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  2. 2 Scroll down the menu and select Add to Home Screen .
  3. 3 Tap Add in the top-right corner. Launch the app from your iPhone Home Screen!

Reset All Hole Logs?

This will uncheck all 19 holes, clear all moisture readings, and delete all notes for today's log.
